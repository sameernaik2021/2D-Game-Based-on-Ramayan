How to play
1. Move to the stone collection area on the left side of the coastline.
2. Press E to collect one stone.
3. Carry the stone to an empty, glowing bridge position and press E again to place it.
4. Complete each group of three bridge pieces to unlock the next section.
5. Press C to change your companion and H to activate Hanuman's strength.
6. Finish all three construction puzzles and cross the completed bridge to reach Lanka.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move, E to interact, H for strength, C for companions.
